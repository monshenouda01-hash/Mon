07 — FAQ

Questions before we start

How long does a project take?

A simple business or portfolio website usually takes one to two weeks. A full management system takes longer. After we talk, I give you a clear timeline before any work begins.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the pages, features and design you need. Send me a short brief and I will reply with a fair quote, so there are no surprises later.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every project is built responsive and tested on phones, tablets and desktops.

Can you add features or fix things after launch?

Yes. I stay available after launch for fixes, updates and new features.

What do you need from me to start?

Your idea, who it is for, and any content or branding you already have (logo, text, photos). If you have none of these yet, we can work it out together.
